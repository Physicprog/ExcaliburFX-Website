ExcaliburFX

Speed-up your workflow with ExcaliburFX. Access to a wide range of tools, presets, expressions and SFX libraries, and more to help you create easier your next project.

Presets Curves

Save and apply custom Bézier curves to keyframes. Copy and paste graph data directly across timeline selections.

Lives Curves

Apply curve to keyframes directly.

Work tab

Edit the layers on your timeline.

Presets and Plugins library

Save your most used presets and plugins for easy access.

Effects builder

Unique tools to work faster.

Colors tab

Choose from a wide range of colors or colors presets, color the layer or copy the #HEX code.

SFX Library

Preview and add sound effects to your project. Or import your own. You can always change the default SFX.

SFX Effects

Apply effects, fade in/out and wave to your sound. You can change fades settings at any time.

Expression Library

Apply expressions to your layers with the 80+ pre-built expressions. You can make a save your own expressions!

Command chain

Apply a series of blocks to your layers. It will be rendered and placed on your timeline with the selected blocks.

Command chain presets

Save your own command chains..

Settings Tab

Customize your excaliburFX interface: change themes, disable unused menus, and organize projects efficiently.
