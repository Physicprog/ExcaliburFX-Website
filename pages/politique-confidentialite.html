Processed Data and Purposes

If you use the contact form, the fields transmitted are your email address, your message, and, if you fill it in, your name. These data are used to receive and process your request, then to respond to it.

The legal basis retained to respond to a request initiated by you is the legitimate interest of the publisher to manage contact requests. Do not transmit sensitive data in the form.

Recipients and Service Providers

The form transmits the entered information to Web3Forms so that the message is forwarded to the publisher. The site's host may also process technical data necessary for the provision and security of the site, according to its configuration.

The Web3Forms service provider is a recipient located outside the site. Before publication, the publisher must verify and indicate here their subcontracting conditions, their potential sub-contractors, the countries of processing, the applicable guarantees for transfers outside the EEE and their retention periods. The host must also be identified in the legal notices.

Retention Period

The informations are retained for the duration necessary to process the request and its follow-up, then deleted, unless there is a legal obligation or necessity to defend a right. Any copies that may be retained by Web3Forms or the messaging service depend on their settings and conditions; the publisher must verify and document these before publication.

Your Rights

Under the conditions provided by the regulation, you can request access to your data, their correction or deletion, the limitation of processing, or you can oppose it. To exercise your rights, contact excaliburfx@outlook.fr. You can also submit a complaint to the CNIL.

Cookies and Trackers

The site's code does not implement any advertising tool or audience measurement tool nor any local storage. The operation of the host and Web3Forms must be checked separately. Consult the cookie policy for available information.

Automated Decisions

No decision producing a legal effect on your behalf is made by the site based on an automated processing.
